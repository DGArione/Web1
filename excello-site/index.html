(04)How we work

An integrated development model

01

Concept & design

We begin with your brief, the site and the climate. Our architects and interior designers work as one studio so the plan, the light and the finishes are resolved together.

02

Engineering & approvals

Structural, MEP and sustainability engineering are coordinated in-house, with approvals and cost planning handled before the first foundation is poured.

03

Procurement & construction

Operating as both developer and construction manager gives us direct oversight of quality, programme and budget across the entire build.

04

Handover & management

We stay after the keys are handed over. At Aathavan, Excello directly manages the property for the first two years following completion.
